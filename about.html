Our Story

We make walls worth looking at.

AH Decals is a wall-décor studio born in Kota, Rajasthan — printing custom canvas, wallpapers and decals that help homes and businesses tell their own story.

Kota ·
Rajasthan ✦
How We Started

A small print shop with a big idea.

AH Decals began on the first floor of JanakDeep Complex in Gumanpura, Kota — a single printer, a roll of vinyl and a belief that good design shouldn't be reserved for galleries.

Today that idea has grown into a full studio producing custom canvas, large-format wallpapers and easy peel-and-stick decals — shipped to homes and businesses across the country, and powered by a growing network of branches and franchise partners.

Everything is printed in-house, checked by hand, and made to your exact size. Your wall, your way.
